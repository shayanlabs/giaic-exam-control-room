# 40. Part 4 — Choosing + the Open Path

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Web home choose karne ka faisla iss baat pe hota hai ke kaam kya touch karta hai — khaas kar data sensitivity aur compliance. Aur ek "open path" bhi hai jo kisi ek vendor ke cloud pe depend nahi karta.

Socho jaise ek furnished flat kiraye pe lena bnisbat khud ghar banane ke. Furnished flat (vendor product) fauri sahulat deta hai — sofa, TV, sab tayyar — lekin ye kisi aur ka ghar hai, tum rules follow karte ho. Apna ghar banana (open path) mehnat mangta hai, lekin control aur azadi poori tumhari hoti hai.

Regulated data ke sath vendor surface pe kaam karne se pehle likhi hui compliance confirmation chahiye — sirf zubani yaqeen dahani kaafi nahi.

## Zaroori Concepts

### Pick a home by what the work touches
Agar kaam regulated data, internal credentials, ya high-stakes actions se juda hai, to compliance aur control ki zaroorat sab se pehle aati hai.

### Regulated data needs compliance in writing first
Regulated data ko vendor surface mein daalne se pehle likhi hui compliance posture confirm karo. Verbal assurance kaafi nahi.

### A vendor sells you a spine
Zyada tar commercial web-agent products tumhe ek convenient, pre-built spine aur harness dete hain. Valuable hai, lekin ye ek qisam ka lock-in bhi hai.

### The open path makes you build one
Open path mein shuru mein zyada mehnat lagti hai (tum khud spine aur harness banate ho) lekin ownership aur switch karne ki azadi bani rehti hai.

### What the open path actually looks like
Vendor cloud ke bina do asal doors hain. **OpenWork** ek open-source desktop co-worker hai jo kisi self-hosted worker ya org ke shared cloud workers se connect ho sakta hai — kaam abhi bhi kisi doosri machine pe hota hai, lekin ab wo infrastructure tumhare apne control mein hai. **OpenCode**, repo-attached kaam ke liye, scheduling ka jawab khud tumhare control ke scheduler se deta hai — cron ya scheduled GitHub Actions job — koi vendor cloud, koi plan tiers nahi.

### The honest trade, stated plainly
Extra setup mehnat ke badle open path do cheezein deta hai jo koi vendor nahi bech sakta: **custody** (data tumhare control ki machines pe) aur **choice** (kaunsa model tumhare prompts dekhta hai, aur switch karne ki azadi). Ye har kisi ke liye sahi nahi — ek solo consultant jo Friday tak brief deliver karna chahta hai shayad vendor ka ready-made spine hi chahega, jabke ek NGO jiski policy donor records third-party platform pe allow hi nahi karti, uske paas open path ke siwa koi option nahi, chahe unke paas koi engineer na ho. Wahan yehi sach kehna zaroori hai: ye path subscription fee ko operator ki mehnat se badalta hai — koi na koi setup, updates, aur jis subah system toote uski zimmedari uthayega. Custody free nahi, operations mein uski keemat chukani parti hai.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?

Course vendor-neutral, ownership-preserving path ko tarjeeh deta hai jab kaam is mehnat ke qabil ho — exam expect karta hai tum jaano kab kaunsa choice sahi hai.
