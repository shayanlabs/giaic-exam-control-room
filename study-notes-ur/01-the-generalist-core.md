# 1. The generalist core

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Ek badi kitchen sochlo. Pehle zamane mein, ek boss 8 cooks ko instructions deta tha, aur cooking mein time lagta tha, is liye ye ratio chal jata tha. Phir magic ovens aur robot helpers aa gaye — ab har cook 5 guna zyada food bana sakta hai. Ab boss ka masla ye nahi ke cooking slow hai, balke ye hai ke 20 cooks ko kya banana hai ye decide karna aur clearly samjhana slow ho gaya hai.

Bilkul yehi cheez coding agents ke aane se hui. Software banana bohot fast ho gaya, lekin "kya banana hai" decide karna aur usko clearly likhna naya bottleneck ban gaya. Isko **intent bottleneck** kehte hain.

Generalist core asal mein chaar jobs hain jo team ki tarah kaam karti hain: ek banda decide karta hai AI ne kya karna hai, doosra us AI worker ko actually banata hai, teesra poori company design karta hai jo bohot saare AI workers use karti hai, aur chautha in sab ko real computers par chalata rehta hai. Jab ek hi insaan customer ki company ke andar ye chaaron kaam karta hai, market usay **Forward Deployed Engineer** kehta hai.

Yaad rakhna: syntax likhna to AI khud handle kar rahi hai, magar judgment, specification aur deployment automate nahi ho sakte — ye machine ke saath saath upar chadhte jaate hain. Ye chaaron roles usi ladder ki seats hain.

## Zaroori Concepts

### Intent bottleneck
Purana ratio tha 1 product manager har 8 engineers ke liye, naya ratio hai 1 par 20, kyunke agents ne building fast kar di magar deciding fast nahi hui — is wajah se decision lena hi traffic jam ban gaya.

### Outcome Architect
Ye banda "kya" aur "kyun" ka owner hota hai, khud AI nahi banata balke clear instruction likhta hai ke worker ne kya achieve karna hai aur "acha kaam" kaisa dikhta hai — recipe card likhne wala insaan.

### Digital FTE Builder
FTE matlab Full-Time Equivalent, yani ek poore time ke human worker jaisa AI worker. Ye banda Outcome Architect ki instruction lekar actual AI worker banata hai — yehi is book ka main focus hai.

### AI-Native Co. Architect
Ye insaan poori company design karta hai jo bohot saare Digital FTEs par chalti hai — AI workers aapas mein kaise baat karenge, insaan control mein kaise rahenge, sab yehi decide karta hai.

### Cloud AI Engineer
Ye banda AI workers ko din raat cloud par chalata rehta hai. Banana to sirf aadha kaam hai, zinda aur reliable rakhna doosra aadha hai.

### 4-role pipeline
Simple hand-off hai: Intent → Build → Company design → Production mein chalana.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?

Aage ke tamam topics inhi chaar seats ke andar aate hain, is liye agar ye pipeline aur intent bottleneck samajh na aaya to baaki course random tukdon jaisa lagega.
