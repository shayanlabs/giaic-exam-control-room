# 1. The generalist core

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Dekho, seedhi si baat hai — AI coding agents aane se pehle, ek product manager (jo decide karta tha kya banana hai) 8 engineers ko handle kar sakta tha. Ab wahi banda 20 engineers handle kar raha hai. Kyun? Kyunke ab coding fast hai, AI khud likh deti hai. Toh asal bottleneck ab "kya banana hai" decide karna aur clearly samjhana reh gaya hai, coding nahi. Isko **intent bottleneck** bolte hain — matlab team ki speed ab is baat par depend karti hai ke koi kitni achi tarah decide aur explain kar sakta hai, na ke kitni fast type kar sakta hai.

Is course ke 4 roles isi problem ko solve karte hain — chaar alag logon ka kaam:

1. Ek banda sirf decide karta hai kya chahiye
2. Doosra wo cheez actually banata hai
3. Teesra poori company design karta hai jahan bohot saare AI workers saath kaam karte hain
4. Chautha sab kuch live rakhta hai, running

Agar ek hi banda ye chaaron kaam kisi client ki company mein karta hai, usay market mein **Forward Deployed Engineer** bolte hain — agla topic yahi hai.

## Zaroori Concepts

### Intent bottleneck
Pehle 1 PM = 8 engineers, ab 1 PM = 20 engineers. Wajah simple hai: agents ne building fast kar di, lekin decide karna utna hi slow hai jitna pehle tha — is liye ab deciding hi rukawat ban gayi hai.

### Outcome Architect
Coding nahi karta — sirf likhta hai ke kya chahiye aur "done" kaisa dikhna chahiye.

### Digital FTE Builder
FTE = Full-Time Equivalent, matlab ek poore waqt ke human jaisa AI worker. Ye banda actual worker banata hai — is course ka main focus yehi hai.

### AI-Native Co. Architect
Poori company design karta hai jo bohot saare AI workers par chalti hai.

### Cloud AI Engineer
AI workers ko 24/7 live rakhta hai. Banana aadha kaam hai, chalate rehna doosra aadha.

### 4-role pipeline
Intent → Build → Company design → Run.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?

Baaki poora course inhi 4 seats ke andar fit hota hai — agar ye pipeline clear nahi to aage sab bikhra bikhra lagega.
