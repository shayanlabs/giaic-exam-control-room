# 8. Choosing Your Vertical

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Vertical matlab poore system ka ek khaas version jo ek profession ke liye bana ho — jaise accounting, healthcare, ya kisi ek mulk ka law. Mahinon lagane se pehle tum ek careful check chalate ho. Sabse zaroori rule simple hai: **agar tumhare paas koi real expert nahi jo ruk kar knowledge ka owner bane, to shuru mat karo.** Us expert ke bagair poori cheez ret par khadi hoti hai.

## Zaroori Concepts

### The screen
Ek structured filter jo tumhe shuru mein hi mushkil sawalon ke jawab dene par majboor karta hai: Kya knowledge available hai? Kya legal rights mil sakte hain? Kya real demand hai? Kya sahi partners hain?

### The eight tests
Aath practical checks jo bhaari time ya paisa lagane se pehle real risks samne le aate hain.

### The launch gates
Saaf "go / no-go" points jo us aam ghalti ko rokte hain jahan log banana shuru kar dete hain aur baad mein pata chalta hai ke koi zaroori piece missing hai.

### Committed domain expert ke bagair vertical nahi
Ye non-negotiable rule hai. Committed expert ke bagair koi Outcome Architect ya FDE achi lagne wali demos banayega jo real use mein fail ho jayengi, kyunke knowledge adhoori ya ghalat hogi.

### Wo aik test jo akela sab kuch khatam kar sakta hai
Aath tests mein se ek khaas hai: Test 3 (Expert availability). Agar ye akela test fail ho jaye, poori evaluation foran ruk jati hai — chahe baaki saaton kitne bhi acche kyun na ho. Ye veto power kyun rakhta hai? Kyunke domain expert hi corpus (unka likha hua material) aur twin (unki persona aur judgment) dono ka source hota hai. Real, committed expert ke bagair jo apni knowledge license karne ko tayyar ho, AI workers ke liye train karne ke liye koi sach cheez hai hi nahi — poora trio (corpus, expert twin, builder) gir jata hai. Baaki saat tests risks hain jo manage kiye ja sakte hain, ye ek hard floor hai.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?

Vertical banana aur bechna graduates ke liye paisa kamane ka ek bada zariya hai — exam check karta hai ke tumhe decision process aur expert requirement dono samajh aate hain.
