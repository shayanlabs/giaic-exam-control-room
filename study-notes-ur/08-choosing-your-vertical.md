# 8. Choosing Your Vertical

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Vertical matlab poore system ka ek khaas version, jo sirf ek profession ke liye bana ho — jaise accounting, ya healthcare, ya kisi ek mulk ka law.

Mahino lagane se pehle, ek careful check chalate hain. Sab se important rule seedha hai: **agar tumhare paas koi real expert nahi jo ruk kar knowledge ka owner bane, to shuru hi mat karo.** Us expert ke bagair poori cheez ret par khadi hoti hai.

## Zaroori Concepts

### The screen
Ek structured filter jo tumse mushkil sawal jaldi puchwata hai: Knowledge available hai? Legal rights mil sakte hain? Real demand hai? Sahi partners hain?

### The eight tests
Aath practical checks jo bade time ya paisa lagane se pehle asal risks samne le aati hain.

### The launch gates
Clear "go / no-go" points. In se ye common galti nahi hoti ke tum banana shuru kar do aur baad mein pata chale koi zaroori cheez missing thi.

### Committed domain expert na ho to vertical bhi nahi
Ye non-negotiable rule hai. Ek Outcome Architect ya FDE bina committed expert ke aise demos banayega jo dekhne mein achhe lagenge, lekin real use mein fail ho jayenge — kyunke knowledge adhoori ya galat hogi.

### Wo ek test jo akela sab kuch khatam kar sakta hai
Aath tests mein se ek khaas hai: Test 3 (Expert availability). Agar sirf ye ek test fail ho jaye, poora evaluation wahin ruk jata hai — chahe baaki saatoon tests mein number kitne bhi achhe ho. Ye test itna power kyun rakhta hai? Kyunke domain expert hi corpus (unka likha hua material) aur twin (unki soch, unka judgment) — dono ka source hai. Real committed expert ke bagair, jo apni knowledge license karne ko raazi ho, AI workers ke paas train hone ke liye kuch sach hi nahi bachta — poora trio (corpus, expert twin, builder) gir jata hai. Baaqi saat tests manage kiye ja sakte hain, ye ek hard floor hai.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?
Vertical bana kar bechna graduates ke paise kamane ka main tareeqa hai. Exam dekhna chahta hai ke tumhe decision process aur expert wali sharat dono samajh aati hai.
