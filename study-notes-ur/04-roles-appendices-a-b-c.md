# 4. Roles appendices (A/B/C)

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Ye appendices practical extras hain jo teen roz marra sawalon ka jawab dete hain: resume mein kya likhun taake log yaqeen karein main ye kaam kar sakta hoon? Job interview kaisa hota hai? Real log coding agents ke saath roz kaise kaam karte hain?

## Zaroori Concepts

### 6 resume signals
Ye concrete proof points hain — sirf "mujhe AI aati hai" kehna kaafi nahi. Iski jagah: "ye raha Digital FTE jo maine banaya aur deploy kiya, ye raha specification jo maine likhi, ye raha test suite jo prove karta hai ke ye kaam karta hai." Real evidence claims se behtar hai.

### FDE interview loop
Ye Forward Deployed Engineer jobs ke liye interviews ka aam sequence hai, jis mein technical skill, messy problems ko clear pieces mein todne ki ability, aur real customers ke saath kaam karne ki salahiyat mix hoti hai.

### Cherny ke 5 archetypes
Boris Cherny (jinhone Claude Code banaya) ne 5 aam tareeqe describe kiye jin se log coding agents use karte hain. In patterns ko jaan kar tumhe apna aur teammates ka style samajh aata hai.

### Decomposition round = spec-driven development bol kar
Interviews mein aksar tumse kaha jata hai ke koi messy problem lo aur usay clear steps mein todo jinko agent (ya team) follow kar sake. Ye bilkul wahi skill hai jo acchi specification likhne mein lagti hai — bas time pressure ke neeche zubaan se boli jati hai.

### Live build exercise
Tum interviewer ke saath baith kar wahin agent use karke kuch banate ho. Ye test karta hai ke tum tools drive kar sakte ho, clear instructions likh sakte ho, results check kar sakte ho, aur real time mein masle theek kar sakte ho.

### Exam se bahar bhi ye kyun matter karta hai
"Mujhe AI aati hai" ek jumla hai jo koi bhi keh sakta hai aur koi verify nahi kar sakta. Shipped Digital FTE, uski spec, aur passing test suite wo cheezein hain jo interviewer khud dekh sakta hai. Poora appendix isi idea par khada hai: jab market AI-skills ke claims se bhari ho, evidence hamesha claims se jeetta hai — is liye evidence abhi se banao, zaroorat aane se theek pehle nahi.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?

Sirf job titles jaanna kaafi nahi, exam expect karta hai tumhe pata ho market in skills ko actually kaise test karta hai.
