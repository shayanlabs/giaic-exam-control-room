Ye chapter practical extras deta hai — teen rozmarra sawalon ke jawab:
1. Resume mein kya likhun taake log yaqeen karein mujhe ye kaam aata hai?
2. Job interview mein kya hota hai?
3. Real log coding agents ke saath din-ba-din kaam kaise karte hain?

## Zaroori Concepts

### 6 résumé signals
Ye concrete proof points hain. Sirf "mujhe AI aati hai" likhna kaafi nahi. Iski jagah: "Ye raha wo Digital FTE jo maine banaya aur deploy kiya. Ye raha spec jo maine likha. Ye raha test suite jo prove karta hai ke ye kaam karta hai." Asal saboot dawon se behtar hota hai.

### FDE interview loop
Ye Forward Deployed Engineer jobs ke interviews ka usual sequence hai. Isme technical skill, messy problems ko saaf pieces mein todna, aur real customers ke saath kaam karna — sab mix hota hai.

### Cherny ke 5 archetypes
Boris Cherny (jinhone Claude Code banaya) ne 5 aam tareeqe bataye hain jin se log coding agents use karte hain. Ye patterns pata hone se apni style aur teammates ki style samajhna aasan ho jata hai.

### Decomposition round = spec-driven development zabaan se bola gaya
Interviews mein aksar tumse kaha jata hai ke ek messy problem ko saaf steps mein todo jo agent (ya team) follow kar sake. Ye bilkul wahi skill hai jo achha spec likhne mein lagti hai — bas time pressure ke neeche zabaan se boli jati hai.

### Live build exercise
Interviewer ke saath baith kar tum wahin agent use karke kuch banate ho. Ye test karta hai ke tum tools drive kar sakte ho, clear instructions de sakte ho, results check kar sakte ho, aur real time mein problems fix kar sakte ho.

### Exam se bahar bhi ye kyun matter karta hai
"Mujhe AI aati hai" — ye sentence koi bhi bol sakta hai, koi verify nahi kar sakta. Lekin ek shipped Digital FTE, uska spec, aur uska passing test suite — ye cheezein interviewer khud check kar sakta hai. Poora appendix isi idea par banaya gaya hai: jab market AI-skills ke dawon se bhari ho, saboot dawon se jeetta hai — is liye evidence shuru se banao, zaroorat parne se theek pehle nahi.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?
Sirf job titles yaad hona kaafi nahi. Exam ye dekhna chahta hai ke market asal mein in skills ko test kaise karta hai.
