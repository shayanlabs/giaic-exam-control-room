# 11. FDE AF Model III — the business layer

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Ek pyara sa diagram bill nahi bharta. Ye hissa teen paise wale sawalon ka jawab deta hai:
1. Har layer paisa kahan se kamati hai?
2. Ek profession ko real vertical banne se pehle kya chahiye?
3. Customer ke saath ek successful project dikhta kaisa hai?

## Zaroori Concepts

### Har layer kahan se kamati hai
- Neeche wali layers zyada tar platform aur licensing se kamati hain.
- Layer 2 education aur tools se kamati hai (taqreeban zero extra AI cost).
- Layer 3 domain partnerships aur vertical licensing se kamati hai.
- Layer 4 real customer projects aur Digital FTEs ke chalte rehne se kamati hai.

### The domain trio
Kisi vertical ko real banne ke liye teen cheezein chahiye:
- **Corpus** — knowledge ka jism (rules, standards, protocols).
- **Map** — wo knowledge kaise organize hai taake insaan aur AI dono dhoond sakein.
- **Reflexes** — automatic skills aur behaviors jo us knowledge par actually amal karte hain.

### The contract of success
Har serious customer project ko teen clear numbers chahiye:
- **Baseline** — customer aaj kahan khada hai (measured).
- **Target** — unhe kahan pohanchna hai.
- **Acceptance criteria** — "done aur good enough" ki exact definition.

### KPIs, adoption, evals
Success ka matlab "AI smart lagi" nahi hai. Success business numbers se, real log system use kar rahe hain ya nahi is se, aur sakht tests se measure hoti hai jo prove karein workers achha kaam karte rehte hain.

### Ek worked example (accounting firm)
Ek graduate ka pehla real engagement socho: ek mid-size accounting firm. Baseline honestly measure hoti hai pehle — abhi ek working-paper file taiyaar karne mein 4 ghante lagte hain. Target, likhit mein aur banana shuru karne se pehle tay hota hai: 40 minute. Acceptance criteria kehta hai AI worker ka output pehli baar mein 95% sahi hona chahiye, aur firm ke apne human reviewers ko sign off karna chahiye. Yehi hai contract of success amal mein — koi bhi banana shuru nahi karta jab tak baseline aur target dono kaghaz par na aa jayein. Aakhri manzil ek achha demo nahi — production mein saboot hai: real KPI numbers, staff ka asal mein tool use karna (adoption), aur passing evals jo dikhayein worker waqt ke saath sahi behave karta raha. Teeno matter karte hain, kyunke ek AI worker har technical eval pass kar ke bhi business number nahi hila sakta agar koi usay adopt hi na kare.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?
Model tabhi kaam ka hai jab wo real outcomes aur real paisa bana sake. Exam dekhna chahta hai ke tumhe sirf technical layers nahi, business side bhi samajh aata hai.
