# 11. FDE AF Model III — the business layer

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Sirf khoobsurat diagram se bills nahi bharte. Ye part teen paise wale sawalon ka jawab deta hai: har layer paisa kahan se kamati hai? Kisi profession ko real vertical banne se pehle kya chahiye? Customer ke saath successful project kaisa dikhta hai?

## Zaroori Concepts

### Har layer kahan se kamati hai
Neeche wali layers zyada tar platform aur licensing se kamati hain. Layer 2 education aur tools se kamati hai (almost koi extra AI cost nahi). Layer 3 domain partnerships aur vertical licensing se kamati hai. Layer 4 real customer projects aur Digital FTEs ke ongoing operation se kamati hai.

### The domain trio
Vertical real banne ke liye teen cheezein chahiye: **Corpus** — knowledge ka jild (rules, standards, protocols), **Map** — us knowledge ko is tarah organize karna ke insaan aur AI dono cheezein dhoond sakein, aur **Reflexes** — automatic skills aur behaviors jo us knowledge par act karte hain.

### The contract of success
Har serious customer project ko teen saaf numbers chahiye: **Baseline** — customer aaj kahan hai (measured), **Target** — unko kahan pahonchna hai, aur **Acceptance criteria** — "done aur acha kaafi hai" ki exact definition.

### KPIs, adoption, evals
Success ka matlab "AI smart lag raha tha" nahi hota. Success business numbers se measure hoti hai, is se ke real log system actually use karte hain ya nahi, aur hard tests se jo prove karte hain ke workers waqt ke saath acha kaam karte rehte hain.

### Worked example (accounting firm)
Ek graduate ki pehli real engagement socho: ek mid-size accounting firm. Baseline honestly measure hoti hai pehle — ek working-paper file taiyar karne mein abhi 4 ghante lagte hain. Target, jo building shuru hone se pehle likh kar agree hota hai, hai 40 minute. Acceptance criteria kehta hai AI worker ka output pehli hi baar 95% sahi hona chahiye, aur firm ke apne human reviewers ko sign-off dena hoga. Yehi hai contract of success amal mein — koi bhi banana shuru nahi karta jab tak baseline aur target dono kaghaz par na hon. Finish line acchi demo nahi hoti — production mein proof hoti hai: real KPI numbers, real staff jo tool actually use kar rahe hon (adoption), aur passing evals jo dikhaen worker waqt ke saath sahi behave karta rahe. Teenon zaroori hain, kyunke AI worker har technical eval pass kar sakta hai magar phir bhi business number na hilaye agar koi usay adopt hi na kare.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?

Model tabhi useful hai jab wo real outcomes aur real paise mein badal sake — exam business side check karta hai, sirf technical layers nahi.
