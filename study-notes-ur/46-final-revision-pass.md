# 46. Final Revision Pass

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Exam se pehle aakhri dinon mein naya material seekhna band kar do. Ab focus sirf baqi bache weak spots aur exam ki exact terminology pe hona chahiye.

Socho jaise match se ek raat pehle koi naya shot practice nahi karta — bas apni kamzor grip theek karta hai aur rules ek baar refresh kar leta hai. Yehi in aakhri dinon ka kaam hai — breadth nahi, depth on the weak spots.

## Zaroori Concepts

### Re-study the weakest 10 cards twice
Jo 10 concepts ya questions abhi bhi kamzor lagte hain unhe identify karo aur do baar unse guzro. Is stage pe weak spots pe depth breadth se behtar hai.

### Go through all glossary terms once
Glossary ek tez tareeqa hai ye confirm karne ka ke har official term familiar hai. Bohot se exam questions course ki precise vocabulary pe hi tike hote hain.

### The Glossary and Cheatsheets pages are fast wins right before the exam
Ye pages jaan-boojh kar dense aur high-signal likhi gayi hain. Aakhri pass in par karna sab se acchi last-minute activities mein se ek hai.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?

Aakhri 48 ghanton mein exact terms aur baqi bache weak cards ka last-mile review sab se high-ROI activity hai. Exam course ki precise language ka command reward karta hai.
