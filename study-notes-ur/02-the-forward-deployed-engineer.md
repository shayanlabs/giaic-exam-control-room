# 2. The Forward Deployed Engineer

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Ek normal software engineer door office mein baith kar ek hi tool banata hai aur wo tool sab companies ko bhej deta hai. Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) iska ulta karta hai — bag uthata hai, customer ke office jaata hai, unke saath baith kar unke hi real data aur computers par tool banata hai wahin bethe bethe.

Isko aise samjho jaise ek doctor sirf report parh kar dusre shehar se salah deta hai, aur doosra doctor tumhare saamne baith kar khud check karta hai aur turant ilaj shuru kar deta hai. FDE wo doosra doctor hai.

## Zaroori Concepts

### Palantir origin
Ye job Palantir naam ki company ne 2010s ke shuru mein start ki, unke logon ko "Deltas" kaha jata tha. Unke customers governments aur bade purane organizations thay jinko koi chahiye tha jo messy setup mein ja kar cheezein fast chala de — kabhi kabhi Palantir ke paas normal engineers se zyada FDEs hote thay.

### Vendor lock-in
Zyada tar companies apne FDEs ko sirf apna hi tool sikhati hain, is se customer ek hi vendor se bandh jata hai — jaise wo plumber jo sirf ek brand ki pipes fit karna jaanta ho. Baad mein tool badalna mushkil aur mehnga ho jata hai. Ye book vendor-neutral version sikhati hai.

### Pod of one
Pehle koi company customer ke paas hafton ke liye 5-6 logon ki team bhejti thi. Ab AI workers ki wajah se ek trained insaan akela Digital FTEs ki poori team command kar ke wohi ya usse zyada kaam kar sakta hai — isay "pod of one" kehte hain.

### Third door (freelance)
Zaroori nahi ke tum kisi badi company mein hi jao, yehi skills use kar ke tum freelance bhi kar sakte ho aur seedha customers ko service bech sakte ho.

### Zaroori numbers
Job postings mein **729% ka izafa** ek saal mein hua, matlab demand bohot zyada hai. **$190,000 median salary** strong mid-to-senior pay hai. **MIT ka 95% pilot failure** number batata hai ke 95 mein se 95 custom AI projects company ke andar koi clear financial benefit nahi dikhate — AI smart to hoti hai, masla usko messy real systems mein fit karna hai, aur yehi FDE solve karta hai. **AWS ka $1 billion aur Microsoft ka $2.5 billion** 2026 mein ek hi hafte mein apni FDE teams par invest hua, jo dikhata hai market kitni serious hai.

### Worked example
Ek mid-size accounting firm socho. Vendor-locked FDE ek hi company ka proprietary platform le kar aayega aur firm ko hamesha ke liye us se bandh dega. Vendor-neutral FDE (jo ye book sikhati hai) open aur portable patterns — skills, specs, MCP connectors — par banata hai, taake model ya tool badalne par bhi Digital FTEs kaam karte rahein. Yehi wajah hai ke "vendor-neutral" baar baar dohraya jata hai — captured asset aur real asset ka farq yehi hai.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?

FDE market ka naam hai us poori skill set ka jo ye book customer ki company ke andar sikhati hai — exam mein pochha jayega ye job kahan se aayi, itna acha kyun pay karti hai, aur vendor-neutral skills kyun zaroori hain.
