# 2. The Forward Deployed Engineer

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Ek normal software engineer daftar mein baitha rehta hai, dur se ek tool banata hai, aur wahi ek tool sabko bech deta hai — chahe customer ka kaam kuch bhi ho. Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) ka tareeqa bilkul ulta hai. Ye banda apna laptop utha kar seedha customer ke office pohanchta hai, unke real logon ke saath baithta hai, aur unke asli data par wahi cheez banata hai jo unko chahiye — wahin, us waqt.

Socho ek darzi jo dukaan mein baith kar sabko ready-made size bechta hai, aur doosra darzi jo tumhare ghar aata hai, tumhari body dekh kar naap leta hai aur tumhare liye suit silta hai. FDE wo doosra wala hai.

Ye concept naya nahi — Palantir naam ki company ne early 2010s mein ye role shuru kiya tha, apne logon ko "Deltas" bulate the. Unke clients governments aur purani badi companies thi, jinhe koi chahiye tha jo messy setup mein ja kar cheezein fast chala de. Kabhi kabhi Palantir ke paas normal engineers se zyada FDEs the.

Ab AI workers ki wajah se ek trained banda akela wahi kaam kar sakta hai jo pehle 5-6 logon ki team karti thi — isko **"pod of one"** bolte hain. Aur ye skill sirf naukri ke liye nahi hai, tum apna khud ka kaam bhi kar sakte ho, seedha customer ko service bech sakte ho — isko **"third door"** kehte hain, matlab freelance wala rasta.

## Zaroori Concepts

### Palantir origin
Palantir ne early 2010s mein FDE role invent kiya, apne logon ko "Deltas" bolte the. Government aur old-school companies ke messy problems fast fix karna unka kaam tha.

### Vendor lock-in
Zyada tar companies apne FDE ko sirf apna khud ka tool sikhati hain — is se customer sirf usi vendor par depend ho jata hai, baad mein switch karna mehnga aur mushkil ban jata hai. Ye book vendor-neutral version sikhati hai, kisi ek company se bandhi hui nahi.

### Pod of one
Pehle team bhejni padti thi, ab ek trained human + Digital FTEs mil kar wahi ya usse zyada kaam akele kar dete hain.

### Third door (freelance)
Company join karna zaroori nahi — yahi skills se tum khud ka kaam bhi kar sakte ho, direct customers ko bech sakte ho.

### Important numbers
- **729% rise in job postings**: ek saal mein FDE job ads tak-taqreeban 8 guna barh gaye — matlab demand bohot zyada hai.
- **~$190,000 median salary**: strong mid-to-senior level pay, top log usse bhi zyada kamate hain.
- **MIT ka 95% pilot failure number**: 100 mein se taqreeban 95 company internal AI projects koi clear faida nahi dikhate — AI to smart hoti hai, mushkil hissa hai usay real messy systems mein fit karna. Yahi FDE ka asal kaam hai.
- **AWS $1 billion + Microsoft $2.5 billion**: 2026 mein ek hi hafte, dono cloud giants ne apni FDE teams mein total $3.5 billion daale — market kitna serious hai, is se pata chalta hai.

### A worked example
Ek mid-size accounting firm socho. Vendor-locked FDE apni company ka bandha hua platform le kar aayega, firm hamesha ke liye usi se jud jayegi. Vendor-neutral FDE (jo ye book sikhati hai) open aur portable patterns par banata hai — skills, specs, MCP connectors — taake model ya tool baad mein badle bhi to firm ka kaam chalta rahe. Yehi wajah hai "vendor-neutral" baar baar dohraya jata hai.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?
FDE market mein us poore skill set ka naam hai jo ye book sikhati hai jab tum customer ki company ke andar kaam karte ho — exam mein role ki origin, salary aur vendor-neutral wali baat clear honi chahiye.
