# 45. Marathon weekends

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho
15 se 30 August tak Zia Khan ke saath teen intensive marathon weekends honge. Tumhe ek section choose karna hai — A, B, ya C — aur live sessions attend karni hain deep practice aur clarification ke liye.

Ye live contact hours high-leverage hain — sawal real-time mein clear hote hain, weak spots saamne aa jaate hain. Serious student inhe miss nahi karta.
