# 43. Build the question bank

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho
Course ke pages mein pehle se hi bohot saare ready-made sawal chhupe hain — "Check Yourself," "Test Your Understanding," aur Flashcards ki shakal mein. Tumhara kaam bas itna hai ke inhe ek jagah, ek master bank mein jama kar lo, taake yaad rakhne ki ummeed pe na baitho, systematically practice karo.

Ye 70+ ready-made sawal already exam format mein likhe hue hain — tumhe kuch bhi scratch se invent nahi karna. Sirf gather karo aur ek jagah rakho.

Ab ye kyun re-reading se better hai? Chapter dobara padhna productive lagta hai, lekin actually weak tareeqa hai — dimaag alfaaz pehchan leta hai aur us pehchan ko samajh samajh leta hai, jo galat hai. Jab tum ek sawal ka jawab dete ho, dimaag ko scratch se jawab banana padta hai — yehi asal exam bhi test karta hai. Isay "active recall" bolte hain, aur ye sabse zyada proven study techniques mein se ek hai. Ready-made sawal ka stack hi asal mein easy banata hai ye karna, sirf notes dobara padhne ki bajaye.

## Zaroori Concepts

### Har "Check Yourself," "Test Your Understanding," aur Flashcards sawal compile karo
Yaadasht ya bikhre notes pe bharosa mat karo. Sab kuch physically ya digitally ek jagah jama karo.

### Roughly 70+ ready-made sawal already exam format mein
Sab kuch scratch se invent karne ki zaroorat nahi. Bohot saara useful practice material pehle se exam-style mein likha hua hai.

### Ye re-reading se kyun better hai
Re-reading productive lagta hai lekin weak learning hai — pehchan ko samajh samajh liya jaata hai. Sawal ka jawab dena "active recall" hai — dimaag ko scratch se reconstruct karna padta hai, exactly wahi jo real exam test karta hai.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?
Active recall sabse high-leverage study activity hai. Bank banana tumhe un exact sawalon se rubaru karta hai jo course important samajhta hai.
