# 43. Build the Question Bank

## Aasan Zabaan Mein Samjho

Course ke pages mein pehle se hi bohot saare ready-made questions maujood hain — "Check Yourself," "Test Your Understanding," aur Flashcards ki shakal mein. Tumhara kaam ye hai ke in sab ko ek jagah, ek master bank mein jama karo, taake tum systematic practice kar sako, bas ye umeed rakhne ki bajaye ke sab yaad rahega.

Socho jaise kisi ne tumhare liye already ek practice-question ka khazana bikhera hua rakha hai poore ghar mein — kuch kitchen mein, kuch bedroom mein. Agar tum sirf ek jagah jamaogay, to test dena aasan aur systematic ho jayega, bnisbat har baar dhoondhne ke.

## Zaroori Concepts

### Compile every "Check Yourself," "Test Your Understanding," and Flashcards question
Memory ya bikhri hui notes pe bharosa mat karo. Inhe physically ya digitally ek jagah jama karo.

### Roughly 70+ ready-made questions already in exam format
Tumhe sab kuch scratch se banana nahi — bohot saara useful practice material pehle se exam-style mein likha hua hai.

### Why this beats re-reading
Chapter dobara padhna productive lagta hai lekin seekhne ka kamzor tareeqa hai — dimaagh alfaaz ko pehchan leta hai aur is pehchaan ko real samajh samajh leta hai, jo ghalat hai. Question ka jawab dena asal "retrieval" force karta hai — dimaagh ko jawab scratch se reconstruct karna parta hai, aur yehi wo skill hai jo real exam test karta hai. Ise "active recall" kehte hain — study techniques mein sab se ziyada support-shuda tareeqon mein se ek. Ready-made questions ka stack hi isse aasan banata hai, sirf notes dobara padhne se ye kaam nahi hota.

## Exam Mein Ye Kyun Aayega?

Active recall sab se high-leverage study activity hai. Bank banana tumhe majboor karta hai un asal questions ka saamna karo jo course ke nazdeek important hain.
