# 46. Final revision pass

## Simple Explanation (like you're 5)

In the last days before the exam you stop learning new material.

You focus on the remaining weak spots plus the exact terminology the exam will use.

## Key Concepts Unpacked

### Re-study the weakest 10 cards twice
Identify the ten concepts or questions you still feel shaky on and go through them twice. Depth on the weak spots beats breadth at this stage.

### Go through all glossary terms once
The Glossary is a fast way to make sure every official term is familiar. Many exam questions hinge on precise use of the course’s vocabulary.

### The Glossary and Cheatsheets pages are fast wins right before the exam
They are deliberately dense and high-signal. A final pass through them is one of the best last-minute activities.

## Why would this be on the exam?
Last-mile review of the exact terms and the remaining weak cards is the highest-ROI activity in the final 48 hours. The exam rewards precise command of the course language.
