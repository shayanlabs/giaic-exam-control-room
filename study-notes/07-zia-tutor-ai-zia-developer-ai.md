# 7. Zia Tutor AI + Zia Developer AI

## Simple Explanation (like you're 5)

There are two helpers that both read from the same official cookbook.

- **Zia Tutor AI** is the friendly teacher. It lives inside the chat apps you already open. It explains the lessons, asks you questions, and remembers where you stopped.
- **Zia Developer AI** is the building partner. It lives inside the coding tools. You tell it the problem; it helps you write the plan and build the AI worker.

In both cases **you bring your own AI brain**. The helpers only bring the knowledge and the teaching or building style. That is why the cost stays almost zero.

## Key Concepts Unpacked

### Teaching lane (Zia Tutor AI)
This is a connector-native app. That means it works inside the normal Claude (or similar) window you already use. Nothing new to install.

### Construction lane (Zia Developer AI)
This is a plugin. It lives inside Claude Code or OpenCode — the places where real building happens.

### Connector-native app vs plugin
- Connector-native = works inside the everyday chat product.
- Plugin = works inside the special coding tool.

### The user brings the model
This is the money secret. The platform does not pay for your AI tokens. You use your own subscription. The platform only serves the trusted knowledge and the thin helpers. That is how it can scale to many people without a huge bill.

## Why would this be on the exam?
These are the two real products students actually use. The exam wants you to know the two lanes and why the cost model works.
