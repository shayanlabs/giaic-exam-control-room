# 10. FDE AF Model II — the one law

## Simple Explanation (like you're 5)

There is one simple rule that keeps the whole cake from falling apart:

**If the same work is done for many customers, move that work down to a shared layer so future customers get it for free.**

If you ignore this rule, every new customer forces you to rebuild the same things again and again. That is expensive and slow.

## Key Concepts Unpacked

### Repeated work moves down
When three different customers all need the same kind of review step or the same domain rule, that piece should not stay as one-off custom work. Clean it up and push it into the shared platform or the vertical.

### The 6 promotion conditions
A practical checklist that decides when something is ready to be shared. The spirit is: the work is general, clean, and valuable to more than one customer.

### Clean-room / opt-in / rewarded promotion
- **Clean-room**: built without any customer’s private secrets.
- **Opt-in**: customers are not forced to take the new shared version.
- **Rewarded**: the people who notice and clean the reusable piece get credit.

### 3+ customers triggers a review
Seeing the same need from three or more customers is usually the signal that a formal “should we share this?” discussion should happen.

## Why would this be on the exam?
Without this law the model never becomes a platform. It stays a collection of one-off projects. The exam wants you to know the promotion discipline.
