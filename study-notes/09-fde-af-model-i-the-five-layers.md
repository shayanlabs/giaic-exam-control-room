# 9. FDE AF Model I — the five layers

## Simple Explanation (like you're 5)

Think of a five-layer cake. You read it from the bottom up.

- Bottom layer (0): the kitchen tools that everyone uses.
- Layer 1: the official cookbook tool that can hold any knowledge.
- Layer 2: the teacher and the building helper.
- Layer 3: a special cookbook for one profession (for example accounting).
- Top layer (4): the real work done for one paying customer.

The platform builds and runs the bottom layers. Graduates build and earn money on the upper layers.

## Key Concepts Unpacked

### Layer 0 — Foundation framework
The basic tools: writing in Markdown, a database that can search by meaning, the standard way for agents to call tools (MCP), and login security. Built once. Nobody above this layer rebuilds it.

### Layer 1 — Content System of Record
Turns any body of knowledge into a trusted, searchable source of truth. The same tool holds the book and later holds accounting rules or medical protocols.

### Layer 2 — Teaching and development ecosystem
Zia Tutor AI and Zia Developer AI. Learning and building at almost zero extra AI cost because each user brings their own model.

### Layer 3 — Vertical ecosystems
Profession-specific packages. Domain experts and partners live here.

### Layer 4 — Customer instances
Real projects for real companies. Digital FTEs are built for that company and success is measured.

### System of Record at three scopes
- **Machinery**: the technical tools.
- **Kernel**: the reusable software component.
- **Instance**: one filled-in copy (this book’s version, or an accounting version, or one customer’s manual).

### Ayesha’s path
A running story of a graduate from Lahore who walks up these layers from learner to someone who can earn at the higher layers.

## Why would this be on the exam?
This is the big picture of both the technology and the business. Almost every other topic is a detail inside one of these layers.
