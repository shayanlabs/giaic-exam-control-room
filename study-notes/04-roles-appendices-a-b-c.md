# 4. Roles appendices (A/B/C)

## Simple Explanation (like you're 5)

These are the practical extras. They answer three everyday questions:
- What should I put on my résumé so people believe I can do this?
- What does the job interview look like?
- How do real people actually work with coding agents day to day?

## Key Concepts Unpacked

### The 6 résumé signals
These are concrete proof points. Not “I know AI.” Instead: “Here is a Digital FTE I built and deployed. Here is the specification I wrote. Here is the test suite that proves it works.” Real evidence beats claims.

### The FDE interview loop
This is the usual sequence of interviews for Forward Deployed Engineer jobs. It mixes technical skill, the ability to break messy problems into clear pieces, and the ability to work with real customers.

### Cherny’s 5 archetypes
Boris Cherny (the person who created Claude Code) described five common ways people use coding agents. Knowing the patterns helps you understand your own style and the style of teammates.

### Decomposition round = spec-driven development spoken aloud
In interviews you are often asked to take a messy problem and break it into clear steps an agent (or a team) can follow. That is exactly the same skill as writing a good specification — just done out loud under time pressure.

### Live build exercise
You sit down with the interviewer and actually build something using an agent right then. It tests whether you can drive the tools, write clear instructions, check results, and fix problems in real time.

### Why this matters beyond the exam
"I know AI" is a sentence anyone can say and nobody can verify. A shipped Digital FTE, its spec, and its passing test suite are things an interviewer can actually inspect. The whole appendix is built around that one idea: in a market flooded with people claiming AI skills, evidence beats claims every time — so build the evidence as you go, not right before you need it.

## Why would this be on the exam?
Knowing the job titles is not enough. The exam expects you to know how the market actually tests for these skills.
