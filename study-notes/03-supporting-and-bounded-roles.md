# 3. Supporting and bounded roles

## Simple Explanation (like you're 5)

Not everyone has to cook the whole meal. Some people only chop vegetables. Some people only taste the food. Some people only clean the kitchen.

The book also honestly says: “We stop teaching here. These other big jobs are real, but they are not what this book is about.”

## Key Concepts Unpacked

### Supporting roles (the helpers)
- **SME as Skill Author**: SME means Subject-Matter Expert — a real accountant, doctor, or lawyer. This person writes the knowledge and rules that the AI workers will use. Without them the AI has nothing true to stand on.
- **Connector / Plugin Engineer**: This person builds the clean pipes so the AI can safely talk to other systems (email, GitHub, databases, etc.).
- **Evals Engineer**: This person designs the tests that prove the AI is actually doing good work and catches mistakes early.
- **Governance Officer**: This person owns the rules about who can see what, who can change what, and how safety is protected.
- **FTE Supervisor**: This is the human who watches the Digital FTEs, checks their work, and steps in when something needs a person.

### Where the book deliberately stops
The book teaches the core skills for building and running Digital FTEs. It does **not** try to make you an expert in three big areas:
- Full **LLMOps** (running giant language-model systems at huge scale)
- Deep **Harness Engineering** beyond the practical level taught later
- Pure **AI Data Engineering** (building massive data pipelines as your main job)

These are real jobs. The book just draws a clear line so you know what is inside the course and what is outside.

### Why the book draws this line on purpose
Each of those three excluded areas (LLMOps, deep Harness Engineering, pure AI Data Engineering) is a full specialization with its own career track and its own books. Trying to teach all of them at "expert" depth would dilute the thing this book is actually training you to be excellent at: the generalist who can specify, build, and deploy a Digital FTE end-to-end. The book would rather make you very good at one clear job than mediocre at five.

## Why would this be on the exam?
You need the full map. The exam wants you to know both the helper roles and the honest boundaries of the book.
